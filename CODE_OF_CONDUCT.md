# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want GrowthSent to be an open, welcoming, and professional community.
Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to treat others with
respect, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, constructive, and specific.
- Welcome different viewpoints and give feedback about the work, not the
  person.
- Assume good intent, ask clarifying questions, and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect privacy and keep private information private.
- Focus discussions on what helps the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, hate speech, or sexualized language.
- Personal, political, or insulting attacks; trolling; or sustained disruption.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately exposing credentials, vulnerabilities, user data, or private
  operational information.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered unprofessional or unsafe.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other spaces where someone represents GrowthSent. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions, issue warnings, limit participation, or ban accounts
when behaviour violates these standards. They will use proportionate action and
protect the privacy of people who report an incident.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to a repository maintainer through the contact
method listed on the repository owner or organization profile. Do not use a
public issue to report harassment, personal information exposure, or a security
problem. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.
